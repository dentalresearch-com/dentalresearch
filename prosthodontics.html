What is Prosthodontics?

Prosthodontics is the branch of dentistry concerned with the restoration and replacement of missing or damaged teeth and associated oral structures.

Prosthodontic treatment may involve fixed restorations, removable prostheses, complete dentures, implant-supported restorations and other approaches designed to restore oral function, comfort and appearance.

For BDS students: A strong understanding of dental anatomy, occlusion, impression procedures, dental materials and treatment planning is essential for learning prosthodontics.

Impression Techniques

Prosthodontics Viva Questions

What is Prosthodontics?

Prosthodontics is the branch of dentistry concerned with restoring and replacing missing or damaged teeth and associated oral structures.

What is a complete denture?

A complete denture is a removable prosthesis designed to replace the natural teeth and associated structures in an edentulous arch.

What is a pontic?

A pontic is the artificial tooth component of a fixed dental prosthesis that replaces a missing natural tooth.

What is an abutment?

An abutment is a tooth or implant that provides support for a prosthetic restoration.

What is centric relation?

Centric relation is a clinically useful reference relationship of the mandible to the maxilla that is independent of tooth contact.

What is osseointegration?

Osseointegration refers to the functional connection between an implant surface and surrounding bone under appropriate clinical conditions.

Patient Education

Patients receiving prosthodontic treatment should understand how to maintain their restoration or prosthesis and why regular dental reviews are important.

  • Maintain good oral hygiene.
  • Clean removable prostheses according to professional instructions.
  • Attend regular dental reviews.
  • Report pain, looseness, fracture or discomfort.
  • Avoid using damaged prostheses without professional assessment.
  • Follow instructions after placement of a new prosthesis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between fixed and removable prostheses?

Fixed prostheses are designed to remain attached to teeth or implants, whereas removable prostheses can be removed by the patient for cleaning and maintenance.

What is a dental bridge?

A dental bridge is a fixed prosthetic restoration that replaces one or more missing teeth using suitable supporting structures.

What is a denture?

A denture is a removable prosthesis used to replace some or all missing teeth and associated oral structures.

Why are impressions important in prosthodontics?

Impressions help reproduce relevant oral structures so that accurate casts and prosthetic restorations can be fabricated.

Can dental implants replace missing teeth?

Dental implants may be used as part of tooth replacement for appropriately selected patients after comprehensive clinical assessment and treatment planning.
