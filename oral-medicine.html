What is Oral Medicine?

Oral Medicine is a specialized field of dentistry concerned with the diagnosis and non-surgical management of diseases affecting the oral cavity and the oral manifestations of systemic diseases.

It connects oral health with general health and involves clinical examination, diagnostic investigations, differential diagnosis, pharmacological management, and coordination of care when necessary.

Clinical Approach to Oral Lesions

A systematic clinical approach is important when evaluating an oral lesion. Important components include:

  • History taking
  • Extraoral examination
  • Intraoral examination
  • Description of the lesion
  • Assessment of site, size, shape, colour and surface
  • Palpation and assessment of consistency
  • Relevant investigations
  • Differential diagnosis
  • Definitive diagnosis and appropriate management
